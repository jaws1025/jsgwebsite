Our why

Why we exist

I've seen recruiting go wrong from the inside. Spray-and-pray submissions, recruiters who vanish after the fee clears, candidates strung along for weeks. I started this firm to do it right.

That means a sniper approach: a handful of people who actually fit, every time. It means telling you the truth, even when it's not what you want to hear. And it means we're selective. We work with clients and candidates who respect our time and share our values.

For employers

Working with us

Hiring is too important for a black-box process. Here is how to engage, how a search runs, and what you get.

Ways to engage

Engaged Search

For leadership and other hard-to-fill roles. A committed, prioritized process with regular market updates and a defined shortlist.

Contingent Search

For active openings where you pay on results. The same vetting and candor, and you only engage when we deliver people worth meeting.

Consulting

Contract and contract-to-hire. Cover a project spike, a leave, or a gap while you hire. Vetted talent that can start fast, with the option to convert to permanent.

How a search runs

01. Define the win

Align first

Role, timeline, and must-haves. We ask the questions that surface what this hire truly needs to own before the search starts.

02. Reach the market

Curate, don't collect

Straight to our network and beyond the applicant pool. You get a shortlist worth your time, not a stack to triage.

03. Hire with confidence

Through onboarding

We stay in it through offer, close, and the first weeks. The job isn't done until your hire is succeeding.

What you get

Direct, honest communication

Consistent updates on the market and the search, and real feedback after every interview from both sides. No phantom roles, no surprises.

Quality over volume

Every submission is vetted and intentional, and worth the time it takes to review.

Founder-led, rooted in the industry

You work directly with the person doing the search, not a handoff to a junior. Deep relationships on the plant floor.
