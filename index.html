Our why

Why we exist

I've seen recruiting go wrong from the inside; the spray-and-pray submissions, the recruiters who disappear after the fee clears, the candidates strung along for weeks. I started this firm to do it the right way instead.

We don't send 100 profiles and hope one sticks. We use a sniper approach: a handful of people who actually fit, every time.

We'll tell you the truth, even when it's not what you want to hear. That's what earns trust, and trust is the only thing that makes a search worth running.

I built this firm to prove you can win in this business without sacrificing work-life balance, so we can actually give back to our community. We're here to build relationships and create "this must be too good to be true" moments, not transactions.

We don't work with everyone, and we don't intend to. We work with candidates and clients we like, who respect our time, and who share our values.

For employers

Working with us

Hiring is too important for a black-box process. Here are the ways to engage, and what you get.

Ways to engage

Engaged Search

For leadership and hard-to-fill roles across construction, plant operations, and technical sales. A committed, prioritized process with regular market updates and a defined shortlist.

Contingent Search

For active openings where you pay on results. The same vetting and candor, and you only engage when we deliver people worth meeting.

What you get

Transparent communication

Consistent updates on the market and the search. You always know where things stand.

Quality over volume

Every submission is vetted and intentional, and worth the time it takes to review.

Real, direct feedback

After every interview, both sides hear what actually happened. No phantom roles.

Rooted expertise

Deep relationships across manufacturing and construction companies. We know the seats, the plant floor, and the jobsite.

Founder-led

You work directly with the person doing the search. Not a name on the proposal and a handoff to a junior.

Through onboarding
