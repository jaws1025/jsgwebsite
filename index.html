Hiring is too important for a black-box process. Here are the ways to engage, and what you get.

Ways to engage

Engaged Search

For leadership and hard-to-fill roles like controllers, finance directors, and accounting managers. A committed, prioritized process with regular market updates and a defined shortlist.

Contingent Search
